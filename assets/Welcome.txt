📢 BVMASH QUIZ CLUB

Official Announcement

Dear Students,

We are pleased to announce the launch of the BVMASH QUIZ CLUB, a school-level quiz initiative designed to encourage knowledge, critical thinking, healthy competition, and teamwork among students of Classes 3–10.

 *You are requested to join your house Group in the community..*

The quiz will be conducted in three rounds, with participants competing individually in the initial round before progressing to the subsequent stages.

🏫 Houses Participating:
• Jasmine House
• Lily House
• Lotus House
• Daffodil House

📝 Round 1
• Date: 16 September 2026
• Mode: Offline
• Format: OMR-based MCQ examination
• Number of Questions: 30
• Participation: Individual

📋 Registration
Students interested in participating will be required to register through the official registration portal. The registration form will collect the necessary participant details for organizing the competition.

The official BVMASH Quiz Club Roll Number will be assigned by the organizers and communicated to participants before Round 1.

All participants are requested to provide accurate information and carefully follow the instructions issued by the organizers and the school.

Further details regarding the subsequent rounds, rules, schedule, qualification, and other arrangements will be communicated through the official channel.

We look forward to enthusiastic participation from all four houses.
